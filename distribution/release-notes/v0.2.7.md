## Fixes

⌨️ Shortcuts follow your keyboard layout. On AZERTY, QWERTZ, Dvorak and other layouts, a letter or digit in a shortcut is the key marked with it: Ctrl+Shift+W is the key marked W. Other keys, such as punctuation and arrows, still go by their position. Recording a shortcut in Settings follows the same rule.

## Improvements

✨ When a field in Settings, such as the search box, gets the cursor, its border fades to the accent colour, and fades back when the cursor leaves.

## 繁體中文

## 修正

⌨️ 快速鍵會依照你的鍵盤配置。在 AZERTY、QWERTZ、Dvorak 等配置上，快速鍵中的字母與數字就是印著那個字的按鍵：Ctrl+Shift+W 就是標著 W 的鍵。標點符號、方向鍵等其他按鍵仍依位置判斷。在設定中錄製快速鍵也遵循同樣的規則。

## 改進

✨ 設定中的欄位（例如搜尋框）取得游標時，外框會漸變成強調色；游標離開時再漸變回來。
