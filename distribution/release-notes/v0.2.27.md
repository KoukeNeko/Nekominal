## Fixes

📂 A path written right after a word, with no space in between, opens again with Ctrl+click and from the hints. A label with a full-width colon, like `分組列：C:\shots\a.png`, or a setting like `out=D:\build\app.log`, used to make the label part of the path, and nothing opened. The path now starts at its drive. Full-width brackets inside a path, as in `C:\新資料夾（2）\a.txt`, still belong to it.

## 繁體中文

## 修正

📂 路徑前面緊接著文字、中間沒有空白時，又能用 Ctrl+點擊或 hints 打開了。像 `分組列：C:\shots\a.png` 這種帶全形冒號的標籤，或 `out=D:\build\app.log` 這種設定，以前會把前面的文字也算進路徑，結果什麼都打不開。現在路徑從磁碟機開始算。路徑中間的全形括號，例如 `C:\新資料夾（2）\a.txt`，還是算在路徑裡。
