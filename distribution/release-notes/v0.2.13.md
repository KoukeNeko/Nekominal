## Fixes

⚡ A program that writes its output in big blocks prints at full speed again: 0.2.12 held it to about 30 MB/s, so 20 MB took a second instead of a third of one. Output written a line at a time keeps the lower CPU of 0.2.12.

## 繁體中文

## 修正

⚡ 以大區塊寫出輸出的程式恢復原本的速度：0.2.12 把它限制在約 30 MB/s，印 20 MB 要 1 秒，而不是約 0.3 秒。一行一行寫出的輸出仍保有 0.2.12 較低的 CPU 用量。
