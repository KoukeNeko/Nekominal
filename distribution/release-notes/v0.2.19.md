## New

🔔 Nekominal looks for a newer version by itself: 30 seconds after it starts, then once a day. When it finds one, a notification says so, once per version, and clicking it opens the About page, where you can update. The switch "Check for updates automatically" on the About page turns this off.

📦 A copy installed with the installer (MSI) now updates itself from the app the way the Scoop copy does, and the programs in your tabs keep running through the update. Nekominal downloads the installer of the new version, checks it against the checksum published with the release, and runs it. If the install fails, Windows puts the old version back and your tabs come back on it. Scoop is still the recommended way to install.

## Fixes

🪟 Full screen keeps the Mica or acrylic backdrop and no longer flickers when what is behind the window redraws. Thanks to @MaoliWolf (#1). Win+arrow keys no longer shrink a full-screen window to half the screen or maximize it.

📁 A zip copy now adds "Open in Nekominal" to Explorer's menu on its first start, as the switch in Advanced, on by default, says it does. It does not replace a menu entry another copy (Scoop's or the installer's) has written.

## 繁體中文

## 新功能

🔔 Nekominal 會自己檢查新版本：啟動 30 秒後檢查一次，之後每天一次。有新版本時會跳出通知，每個版本只通知一次，點一下會開啟「關於」頁，可以在那裡更新。「關於」頁的「自動檢查更新」可以關掉這個功能。

📦 用安裝程式（MSI）安裝的版本，現在也能像 Scoop 版一樣在程式裡更新，更新時分頁裡的程式會繼續執行。Nekominal 會下載新版本的安裝程式，和 release 上公布的檢查碼比對後再執行。安裝失敗時 Windows 會還原舊版，分頁也會接回舊版。我們仍然建議用 Scoop 安裝。

## 修正

🪟 全螢幕時會保留 Mica 或壓克力背景，後面的視窗重畫時也不會再閃爍。感謝 @MaoliWolf（#1）。在全螢幕按 Win+方向鍵，也不會再把視窗縮成半個螢幕或變成最大化。

📁 免安裝的 zip 版第一次啟動時，就會在檔案總管的右鍵選單加入「在 Nekominal 開啟」，和「進階」裡預設開啟的開關一致。如果其他版本（Scoop 版或安裝程式版）已經寫入了這個選單，不會去取代它。
