## Fixes

🔤 Editing Devanagari and other Indic text at the prompt no longer leaves stray characters on screen. When you typed a character just before a virama (`्`), Nekominal placed the rest of the line one column away from where the shell had drawn it, and after the shell redrew the line, an old letter was left behind. Nekominal now groups the letters around a virama the way the Windows console does. (#3)

## 修正

🔤 在提示字元編輯天城文等印度系文字時，不會再留下多餘的字元。之前在 virama（`्`）前面打字，後面的文字會比 shell 畫的位置偏一格，shell 重畫那一行後，畫面上就留下一個多出來的字母。現在 Nekominal 對 virama 前後字母的組合方式和 Windows 主控台一致。(#3)
