## Improvements

☑️ The question that asks before you close a window, tab or pane with running sessions now has a "Don't ask again" checkbox. Tick it with the pointer, or with Tab and then Space, and choose Close: Nekominal closes and stops asking. Cancel changes nothing, even with the box ticked. To bring the question back, turn on "Confirm before closing" in the Terminal page of the settings, which is the same setting.

## 繁體中文

## 改進

☑️ 關閉有執行中工作階段的視窗、分頁或窗格之前的確認，現在有「不再提醒」勾選框。用滑鼠，或用 Tab 再按 Space 勾選，然後按「關閉」：Nekominal 會關閉，之後不再詢問。按「取消」不會改變任何設定，就算勾了也一樣。要讓這個確認回來，到設定的「終端機」頁打開「關閉前確認」，它們是同一個設定。
