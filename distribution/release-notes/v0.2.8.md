## Fixes

📜 The Scrollback lines setting now takes effect. A pane used to stop at its own memory limit, so a higher setting did nothing past about 23,000 lines at 120 columns, and a wide pane kept even fewer than the default. Now the pane keeps the number you set, up to 400 columns wide, and open tabs pick up a change at once. The highest value is 100,000.

📊 The note at the bottom of the context card fits on one line.

## 修正

📜 「捲動歷史行數」設定現在會生效。之前每個窗格有自己的記憶體上限：在 120 欄寬時，超過約 23,000 行的設定都不會生效，寬的窗格甚至保留不到預設的行數。現在窗格會保留你設定的行數（寬度最多 400 欄），開著的分頁也會立即套用。最大值為 100,000。

📊 脈絡卡片底部的說明改為一行顯示。
