## Improvements

🐱 A tab whose program has no icon of its own, and isn't one of the programs with a matching glyph, now shows the Nekominal icon instead of the plain terminal glyph. Programs with their own icon, like PowerShell, keep theirs.

## Fixes

🪟 The updater's window no longer shows a second set of minimize and close buttons over its own. During a Scoop update this window is drawn by the version you are updating from, so you will see the fix when you update from 0.2.24 to a later version.

## 繁體中文

## 改進

🐱 如果分頁裡的程式沒有自己的圖示，也不在有對應圖示的程式之列，分頁現在會顯示 Nekominal 的圖示，不再是一般的終端機圖示。像 PowerShell 這種有自己圖示的程式，還是用原本的圖示。

## 修正

🪟 更新視窗不會再多出第二組最小化和關閉按鈕。Scoop 更新時，這個視窗是由你更新前的那個版本畫的，所以要從 0.2.24 更新到之後的版本時，才會看到這個修正。
