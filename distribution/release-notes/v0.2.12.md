## Lighter while printing

⚡ A flood of output costs much less CPU: printing 20 MB of plain text now takes Nekominal about 1.8 s of CPU against 2.8 s for Windows Terminal, down from 5.5 s, in the same time.

- Plain ASCII text skips the work that joins characters into one (accents, emoji), which only other characters need.
- Output is read and handed to the emulator without waking a thread for every line ConPTY writes.
- While output streams, the window draws at most 60 frames a second instead of at every refresh of a fast screen. Right after a key it draws at once, so typing feels the same.

## 繁體中文

## 輸出時更輕量

⚡ 大量輸出時用的 CPU 少很多：印出 20 MB 純文字，Nekominal 現在約用 1.8 秒 CPU（之前 5.5 秒），Windows Terminal 為 2.8 秒，花的時間相同。

- 一般 ASCII 文字略過把多個字元合成一個（重音符號、emoji）的處理，那只有其他字元需要。
- 讀取輸出並交給模擬器時，不再為 ConPTY 寫出的每一行喚醒一次執行緒。
- 輸出持續湧入時，視窗每秒最多畫 60 幀，不再跟著高更新率螢幕每次刷新都畫。按鍵後會立即畫，打字手感不變。
